## Status discipline

Detailed prose does not make a proposal operative. A structurally coherent future dossier could still contain a false or irrelevant claim. A future valid signature could establish control of a key over exact bytes without establishing identity, truth, independence, recipient admission, or permission to execute. Public hosting supplies only rights necessarily provided by GitHub's Terms of Service or applicable law; it does not supply broader reuse, modification, or redistribution permission or establish release readiness.
