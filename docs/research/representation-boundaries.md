# Representation-boundary research

**Status:** exploratory research note  
**Implementation:** none  
**Network dependency:** none required by this document

Internal or neural representation research is parallel to joint publishing and is not an architectural dependency of a serializable dossier.

## Shared question, not shared mechanism

Publishing asks which selected relationships, contrasts, and falsifiers survive conversion into an inspectable symbolic object. Representation research may ask which task-relevant distinctions survive a model's internal transitions or a constrained readout. Similar questions do not establish a common mechanism, make a dossier equivalent to a latent state, or imply that a portable format captures everything a publisher knew.

Readable patterns, geometric similarity, or probe accuracy do not alone establish causality. Any internal-state study must distinguish predictive distributions, hidden activations, caches, trajectories, and interventions rather than joining them by metaphor.

## Retained null hypotheses

- **H0-R1:** relational anchors and negatives add no recovery of consequential distinctions over a high-quality summary under matched reading time.
- **H0-R2:** an internal intermediate representation adds no prediction of meaningful later outcomes over simpler baselines and matched local context.
- **H0-R3:** apparent cross-representation alignment is explained by topic, lexical, or scorer artifacts and does not survive controlled intervention.

Future work should preregister representations, baselines, interventions, hard negatives, and predictive metrics. Positive or negative results belong in their own research record and must not silently become publication-format requirements.
